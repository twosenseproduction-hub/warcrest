Axis & origin convention

glTF is Y-up; the pipeline is Z-up (Blender). Export maps three.js (x, y, z) → pipeline (X = x, Y = −z, Z = y): X right, Y forward, Z up. Origin is floor-center — the model's bounding-box centre in X/Z at its lowest point (Z = 0). Positions are then rescaled so model height matches the export height above. Coords round to the millimetre.
