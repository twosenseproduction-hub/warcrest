How calibration works

Per view: click floor (Z=0), then top of head (Z=model height) to fix the vertical scale, then click the center axis to fix the horizontal origin (X=0 in front, Y=0 in side). Front horizontal → +X to the right; side horizontal → +Y forward. Final Z averages the front and side reads (delta shown). A landmark placed only in front gets X,Z with Y=0 (fine for mid-line joints like chin/hips). Values export rounded to the millimetre for determinism.
