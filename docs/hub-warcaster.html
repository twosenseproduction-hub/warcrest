Warcrest · HUD · Warcaster Hub

One right-hand hub,seven ways to carve it.

A single control concept, styled seven ways. A portrait node anchors the right thumb; tap it (or select on the field) and it becomes the hinge of a radial — the warband fans to its left, abilities arc to its upper-right, Wild Rift's two-wing grammar. The left hand drives navigation — a movement nub plus camera. Every style below keeps that exact anatomy and only changes the material, all fashioned WC3-heavy.

Center · portrait

The selected hero / warband / building. The hub's hinge and the radial's origin.

Left wing · warband

Squad groups — melee, archers, all — to jump and command by type.

Upper-right · abilities

The hero kit: cooldown sweep, level locks, the ultimate furthest out.

Left hand · navigation

Movement nub + camera / recall, so steering never crosses the action thumb.
