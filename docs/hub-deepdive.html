Warcrest · HUD · Deep Dive

Two hubs, developedto the next layer.

A closer pass on the two layouts worth pushing: the Iron Console — slimmed from a screen-eating slab to a single ornate band — and the Grove Radial, grown from one ring into a full command system that changes shape with what you select. Everything is still live HTML/CSS on the shared component kit.

01

Iron Console — the thin band

The original console stacked a 4×3 command card, a selection parchment and the minimap into a block that swallowed roughly a third of a portrait screen. The refinement collapses it to one carved-gold band about 56px tall: framed portrait, name and health at the left, the context commands flowing along a single row to the right, minimap floated back onto the field. Same WC3 grammar and ornamentation — a fraction of the footprint.

Grove Radial — the bloom system

The portrait study showed a single ring. Developed out, the radial becomes the game's whole command surface: the field rests empty, and whatever you tap decides which ring blooms under your thumb — orders for a warband, structures for a worker, ability sigils for your hero. One gesture grammar, three payloads, zero persistent chrome.
